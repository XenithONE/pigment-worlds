# Painted botanical atlas — historical asset

Created with the built-in image generation tool for PIGMENT, September 2026. This is original generated oil-style artwork, not a reproduction or a crop of a game screenshot.

This records the earlier botanical image-card implementation. v15 does not load or use this atlas: [painted-flora.js](../src/render/painted-flora.js) now instantiates closed 3D leaves, petals and stems from [pigment-herbs.js](../src/render/pigment-herbs.js). The source files and prompts remain as production history.

- Selected source: [source-images/flora-atlas.png](source-images/flora-atlas.png), 1254 × 1254 RGBA.
- Former runtime copy, retained but unused by v15: [../public/art/materials/flora-atlas.webp](../public/art/materials/flora-atlas.webp), lossless WebP, 2,369,598 bytes. The original alpha is preserved.
- Quadrants: blue iris/gold wildflowers, pink/ivory/lilac garden, crimson/ochre autumn, cream/blue coastal grasses.
- Earlier renderer: each clump used two crossed textured planes as a middle-distance supplement to closed 3D plants, with world lighting, received shadows, fog, instancing, and camera-distance removal. Those cards were not modeled botanical meshes and have been replaced in v15.
- The source has real transparent alpha and natural holes between leaves. Most interior alpha is 242–252/255, with low-alpha fringe around some contours; the earlier renderer used alpha testing to remove that fringe. A narrow shader border taper and UV inset protected atlas boundaries. Large safe gutters requested in the prompt were not fully followed, so this is not a general-purpose foliage atlas for arbitrary filtering settings.
- The earlier renderer removed cards near the camera and did not cast static card shadows that would remain after their camera-dependent fade. The atlas was unsuitable for extreme close-ups.
- Two built-in extraction edits were rejected because they baked a checkerboard backdrop into RGB. Only the first genuinely transparent result was selected for the earlier implementation and remains in the repository.

## Selected generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production botanical cutout atlas for mid-distance crossed-card vegetation inside a richly painted 3D oil-painting adventure. Generate ONE square 2048x2048 image with a REAL TRANSPARENT RGBA background, arranged as a precise 2x2 atlas. Four separate botanical clusters, one centered inside each quadrant, all entirely isolated with transparent gaps and 8 percent safe padding inside their quadrant. No plant crosses the center horizontal or vertical axis. Top-left: magnificent cobalt/ultramarine blue irises and small golden-yellow wildflowers among dense long dark teal and olive leaves, varied graceful stems, realistic botanical scale. Top-right: Monet-inspired pale rose, ivory and lilac garden flowers with soft sage and viridian long leaves and several delicate pink iris-like blossoms. Bottom-left: autumn red/crimson and ochre/gold wildflower heads amid bronze and dark olive leaves, a few curling decorative grasses. Bottom-right: restrained coastal clump of cream flowering grasses, blue-gray slender leaves and tiny ivory and pale blue flowers. Each is a varied organically asymmetric planted clump seen from human eye level, wider at its leafy base, plants growing upward, full silhouette from low stem base to flower tips, no soil pedestal, no ground plane, no container. ART DIRECTION: painterly realism with exquisite tactile thick oil paint, broad beautifully layered pigment strokes on petals and leaves, rich wet glints and deep colored folds; sophisticated traditional oil-painting brushwork, convincing natural plant anatomy, expressive individual stems and ragged leaf edges, visually dense but readable. Each clump mixes heights and flower scales, with natural transparent holes between stems. Soft neutral directional daylight, colour-rich local shading, no cast shadow outside plants. Silhouette edges are plant silhouettes with genuine transparent alpha: absolutely NO white background, NO black background, NO gray checkerboard painted into image, NO canvas square behind clusters. All four quadrants share same brushwork, scale and lighting. Entire atlas full bleed transparent with four separate cutouts. No labels, text, grid lines, frame, watermark, signature, scenery, rocks or people.
